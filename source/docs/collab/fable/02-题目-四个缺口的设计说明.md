## Project in one paragraph

From round 16 both sides get +1 merit per round; merit is capped at 30. Engine: source/src/bingfa.js (read the CFG block at the top — every number lives there). AI: source/src/bfai.js (αβ + quiescence + check extension; node-budget mode for reproducible tests).

## The four gaps (each becomes ONE independent switch)
- **G1 封锁期的账**: 汉 sees the loss from 破釜 at ply 1 but not the lock-window payback 4–7 plies later → over-defends (loses 1.3–1.9 material before pofu happens). Measured lock value C (deep search, with vs without lock): ≈0 under current rules (40 of 46 positions exactly 0); median 4.5 under candidate “plan A” (lock also blocks upgrades and passives); suggested C ≈ 2.5 + 0.3·min(楚 merit,10), fading linearly with remaining lock turns. Env PFC=4.5 + PFD=1 raised 汉 from 35.0% → 45.3% under plan A (d492bfa). The AI must do this accounting itself FROM CFG (which things the lock blocks, how long), not from hard-coded rule assumptions.
- **G2 军功规划**: income +1/round from round 16 (CFG.merit.autoIncomeFromRound), cap CFG.merit.cap=30 (income above the cap is lost). The AI knows neither. Measured: pawn-first −218 Elo, delaying the rook −46, hoarding until threatened −34, “don't upgrade full-HP lv2+” −16 — the current rook-first greedy policy is locally strong; improvements must be subtle (e.g. don't waste income at the cap; value merit by what it can buy soon; anticipate income when choosing between upgrading now and later).
- **G3 士/象**: almost never upgraded (only when directly threatened); 象 never leave home (齐射/践踏/飞越 0%); 84.6% of mates involve a lv≥2 rook; 74% are a 2+HP piece mating next to the king. Find whether a defender upgrade/use is genuinely worth more than the default in some recurring situations — only if it is real; “no change” is an acceptable answer for G3 if you can show why.
- **G4 引破釜 / 设陷阱**: 汉 should sometimes choose a line that invites 破釜 when the lock window pays more (G1 is the accounting; G4 is the active choice), and 楚 should decline a tempting 破釜 that walks into such a line. This may fall out of G1 automatically — say so if it does, and then G4's switch may be empty.
